Named for the highest peak of the Carpathians, Hoverla greets the first light of day. Its
panoramic shell opens over layered ridgelines — drift to sleep beneath slow-turning
constellations and wake to dawn spilling across the mountains. The interior carries the
craft of its homeland: hand-loomed wool, beech wood, and the warm restraint of the
Carpathian highlands.
